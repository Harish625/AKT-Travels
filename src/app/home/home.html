About AKT Travels

Coimbatore’s Most Trusted Travel Partner for Every Journey

AKT Travels is a Coimbatore-based travel company providing premium vehicle rental services across Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Karnataka. Whether you need an SUV for a family trip, a Tempo Traveller for a group outing, a Force Urbania for a luxury corporate event, or a 45-seater AC bus for a wedding function, we have the right vehicle at the right price.

Every trip we plan is backed by well-maintained vehicles, experienced and courteous drivers, and round-the-clock support — so you can focus on the journey while we take care of the road. From short city transfers to multi-day hill-station tours, AKT Travels is built to move families, friends, colleagues and wedding parties safely and comfortably.
